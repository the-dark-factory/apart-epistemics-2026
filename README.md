# It asked your birthday, then guessed your tax

Code and data for the Apart AI × Epistemics Research Sprint report (13–16 November 2026):
*It asked your birthday, then guessed your tax: knowing what you weren't told is not a model skill.*

Author: Tony Gair, The Dark Factory Ltd. Licence: AGPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE`). The TaxCalcBench-derived returns keep
TaxCalcBench's MIT licence (github.com/column-tax/tax-calc-bench).

### The proven units (SPARK Ada, every theorem proved by GNATprove for every input)
- `wu-fr-abattement`: the French 10 % allowance (minimum 504 €, maximum 14 426 €, never above the salary)
- `wu-fr-bareme`: the French 2025 income tax bands, per part
- `wu-fr-quotient`: the family quotient and its cap (1 791 € per extra half-part; 4 224 € for a single parent)
- `wu-fr-decote`: the décote
- `wu-fr-recouvrement`: the 61 € collection rule
- `wu-fr-ir-compose`: the whole French calculation, from salaries to the tax collected, with the official brochure and
  BOFiP examples proved end to end
- `wu-fact-gate`: the completeness gate. It is never COMPLETE when a required fact is missing, always passes a complete
  input, and names only a fact that is truly absent.
- `wu-fr-ir-edge/fr_ir_main.adb`, `wu-fact-gate-edge/fact_gate_main.adb`: the command-line programs around them,
  generated by the factory and checked by probe (23/23 and 16/16).

The prose specifications from which these units were generated, and the factory that generated them, are not included.

## Reproducing

- **Pre-registered analysis:** `cd harness/epirun && go build && ./epirun analyse -items ../../study/frozen/items.jsonl -run ../../study/runs/<run> -boot 1000`.
  The four-model panel test concatenates the four frontier runs' `calls.jsonl`.
- **Paths:** `items.jsonl` and some files record absolute paths from the machine that ran the study. Rewrite the prefix
  before running, for example with `jq` on `.input` and `.key_file`.
- **Gap probe:** `french/scripts/TAXCALC_TY24/tools/probe/gapprobe.zsh study/frozen/items.jsonl` flags 40/40 missing-fact
  returns and 0/40 complete ones, in about 1.5 s, with no model.
- **Proven gate:** build `fact_gate_main` from `proven/wu-fact-gate-edge/` with GNAT and the carried spec, then run
  `gate-run.zsh study/frozen/items.jsonl ./fact_gate_main`.
- **French scoring:** `score-fr.zsh <runs/model>` against `cases/key.tsv`.

## Limits
See the report's Limitations appendix. In short:
- one sample per item;
- simple returns only;
- the French arm is exploratory and small;
- OpenAI and Google models were not tested.

## Use of AI
Built and run with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) under the author's direction. Claude Opus 5.5 is also one of the
models under test. All scoring is mechanical.
